How to read this. Each card is one day. The running is up top, the gym work under it, and the last line tells you how the session is supposed to feel — that line matters more than the numbers. Blue days build your engine, orange days protect your speed, green days are genuinely easy, and you get one full day off.
Three rules that beat any workout here.
  1. Easy days have to be easy. If you run your easy days medium-hard, your hard days stop working and you just get tired.
  2. Take the day off. You are still growing. That day is when you actually get faster, not the day you ran hard.
  3. Eat and sleep like it's training. Nine hours of sleep and enough food beats every session in this app. Under-eating is the most common way young runners stall out or get hurt.
Pain is different from tired. Sharp pain, pain in one exact spot, or pain that gets worse as you run — stop, and tell your coach or a parent. Nothing here is worth an injury.
One model, not gospel. This is built around one athlete: strong 400 speed, an aerobic engine that is still catching up. Adjust it with your coach based on how you actually recover.
